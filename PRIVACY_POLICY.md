# Privacy Policy for Husky - Job Helper

Effective date: June 16, 2026

Husky - Job Helper is a Chrome extension that helps job seekers evaluate LinkedIn job listings by showing experience and visa sponsorship indicators directly on LinkedIn jobs pages.

## Data Collection

Husky - Job Helper does not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share personal information.

The extension reads visible job listing text on LinkedIn jobs pages only so it can identify years of experience and visa sponsorship signals. This analysis happens locally in your browser.

## Data Storage

Husky - Job Helper does not store user data.

The extension includes a local company data file used to help identify H1B sponsorship context. This file is packaged with the extension and is used locally in the browser.

## Data Sharing

Husky - Job Helper does not share data with third parties.

## Remote Services

Husky - Job Helper does not use external APIs, remote code, analytics services, advertising networks, or tracking services.

## Permissions

The extension runs on LinkedIn jobs pages so it can read visible job listing content and display helpful badges on those pages.

## Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated if the extension's behavior changes. Updates will be posted in this repository.
